# CarDiag DZ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23

CarDiag DZ stores only the information needed to provide its vehicle and diagnostic features.

## Information stored
- Email address used for account authentication.
- Vehicle information that you explicitly save, such as model, nickname, VIN and mileage.
- Diagnostic sessions, fault codes and measurements that you explicitly submit for cloud diagnosis.

## How information is used
Information is used to authenticate you, display your vehicles, maintain diagnostic history and provide requested diagnostic analysis.

## Bluetooth and OBD
Bluetooth is used only to communicate with a compatible ELM327 OBD-II adapter when you start an OBD operation. CarDiag does not use Bluetooth to track your location.

## Sharing
CarDiag does not sell your personal information. Cloud diagnostic requests are sent to the configured diagnostic service only when you request AI-assisted analysis.

## Retention and deletion
Saved vehicles and diagnostic history remain associated with your account until you delete them or request account deletion. A production release must expose a verified account/data deletion workflow before public launch.

## Security
Database access is protected with Supabase Row Level Security and authenticated user policies. Secrets and service-role credentials must never be shipped in the Android application.

## Medical/safety disclaimer
CarDiag is an automotive information and assistance tool. It does not replace qualified mechanics, manufacturer diagnostic equipment or professional repair procedures. Safety-critical faults must be inspected by a qualified professional.
